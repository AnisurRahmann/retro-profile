## title
eyebrow: Ch. 07
title: Ship results, not features
sub: "If it didn't move a number, it didn't matter."

## body
head: Day 1 impact
body: I don't need 3 months to ramp up. At Gerald I found high-impact problems fast: $900K saved on Plaid costs, $96K saved by replacing an SEO agency, $1M+ monthly GMV unlocked through Amazon integration. Every feature I shipped had a clear business metric tied to it.

## body
head: Data over opinions
body: I don't build on hunches. I instrument everything, track every metric, and let data drive decisions. When I cut support time 80%, the eval harness proved it worked before I ever claimed it.

## body
head: Automate dependencies away
body: Paying an agency? I'll build the system to replace it. Engineers bottlenecked on support? I'll build tools that scale the CS team. If something can be automated, it should be.

## quote
q: I don't see engineering as a cost center. I see it as leverage. Every sprint should have ROI.
