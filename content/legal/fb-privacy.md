## Who we are

Build Box is a self-hosted social media scheduling tool (Postiz-based) available at [social.buildbox.host](https://social.buildbox.host). It is operated by Anisur Rahman Shakil, Sylhet, Bangladesh. You can contact us at any time at [shakilwizard@gmail.com](mailto:shakilwizard@gmail.com).

## Information we access

When you connect a Facebook Page or an Instagram Business/Creator account through Meta's login, we receive:

- the Page or account name and ID
- the profile picture
- access tokens for the connected Page or account
- the content you schedule or publish through Build Box
- comments on those posts
- Page and account insights

## How we use your data

We use this data only to:

- schedule and publish the posts you create
- let you reply to and manage comments on those posts
- show analytics for the accounts you chose to connect

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Data is sent only to Meta's APIs to perform the actions you requested in Build Box.

## Storage and security

Your data and access tokens are stored on our own server. Access to them is restricted to the operator.

## Retention

We keep your data only while the account is connected. It is deleted when you disconnect the account or ask us to delete it.

## Revoking access

You can revoke Build Box's access at any time:

- **Facebook:** Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → remove "Build Box".
- **Instagram:** Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites → remove "Build Box".

## Deletion requests

Want everything deleted? The steps are on the [Data Deletion page](/fb-data-deletion).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with an updated date.
