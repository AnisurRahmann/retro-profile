## How to delete your data from Build Box

1. **Remove the app:** Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → select "Build Box" → Remove.
2. **Email us:** send a message to [shakilwizard@gmail.com](mailto:shakilwizard@gmail.com?subject=Data%20deletion%20request) with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name of the connected Page or account.

## What happens next

We delete all stored data and access tokens for that account within 30 days and confirm the deletion by email.
