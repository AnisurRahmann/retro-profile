# T2 hand-off

Status: done

## What I built

- `generateWeatherMap(params)`: the pure, seeded, tileable 128×128 RGBA8 generator (R coverage, G type,
  B precipitation, A 255). It follows the brief's formula verbatim —
  `cov = clamp(n·1.4 − (1 − coverage)·1.1, 0, 1)` — over per-type, seeded, wrapped value noise:
  - **stratus** — one very low octave (freq 3), per-map z-scored to level 0.75 ± 0.09σ: a smooth, near-uniform
    deck with no holes at 0.95 and a thin, broken deck at low coverage.
  - **cumulus** — the product of two wrapped octaves (freq 4 envelope, freq 11 patches), turned into per-map
    **percentiles** and lifted by `n = 1 − 0.9·(1 − percentile)^2.15`: clear gaps, dense clusters.
  - **cumulonimbus** — the cumulus field plus 1–2 seeded gaussian cells (`s = exp(−3·(d/r)²)`, radius 0.12–0.2 of
    the map, toroidal distance), raising coverage to 1 and stamping the type to cumulonimbus inside.
  - **forcedCell** — the same gaussian stamping coverage ≥ 0.95 at its centre and type 0.5 (a dense cumulus cell),
    wrapped across the edges; radius 0 is a point cell (one texel, no NaN).
  - **precipitation** — `B = param × smoothstep(0.45, 0.95, cov)`: zero at param 0, at full strength only under
    the densest cells, never above the param.
- The per-map **calibration** is the load-bearing decision: percentiles (patchy) and z-scoring (stratus) pin the
  histogram, so `coverage` is what types.ts says — the map's mean target — for *every* seed, not just lucky ones.
  (Uncalibrated value noise varied 0.16–0.44 in mean across seeds, and seed 1 — the default — failed the window.)
- `createWeatherMap(gl, params)`: the WebGL wrapper. RGBA8 upload with LINEAR filtering and REPEAT wrap
  (the generator wraps, so there is no seam); `update(dt)` advances `offset` in place and wraps into [0, 1);
  `setParams` always refreshes `velocity` but **regenerates only when a field param changed** (type, coverage,
  precipitation, seed, forcedCell — the wind only scrolls, so wind-only changes never re-upload).
- `windVelocity(kmh, fromDeg)` (unchanged math) + `WIND_TIMELAPSE = 15` (was 6), documented in the file.

## Decisions (for M1/T8 to carry into docs/DECISIONS.md)

1. **The weather map is calibrated per map.** The patchy field is percentile-ranked and the stratus deck z-scored,
   so the coverage param means the same thing for every seed (types.ts documents it as "the map's mean coverage
   target"). The pattern stays the seed's; only the histogram is pinned. Measured (bytes, every seed): cumulus@0.38
   mean 0.377, 24% clear, 44% cores, ceiling 0.718 (the formula's own cap); stratus@0.95 mean 0.94–0.95,
   deck 0.66–1.0.
2. **The type channel is per texel, HZD-style.** Stratus decks are 0, cumulus fields 0.5; a cumulonimbus sky is a
   cumulus-grade field with the 1–2 seeded cells stamped to 1 (the towers), and a forcedCell stamps 0.5 even over
   stratus (the contract says "a dense cumulus cell"). T4 should shape height gradients from the map's G.
3. **`WIND_TIMELAPSE` 6 → 15.** 15 km/h at 15× is 0.0625 km/s apparent, crossing the 40-km tile in ~10.7 min;
   two frames 10 s apart (T6's drift check) differ by ~2 texels — visible without racing. Per frame at 60 fps the
   scroll is 2.6e-5 uv, far below anything T7's reprojection would smear on.
4. **Wind-only `setParams` never regenerates.** The field doesn't depend on the wind, so dragging the wind slider
   re-scrolls only. A forcedCell that T5 recomputes each second (the field drifts, so its uv moves) does regenerate
   by design — a 128×128 regen costs ~1.5 ms, once per second, only while `cloudAtSun` is on.
5. **The hash folds x and y in multiplicatively.** An XOR-folded draft collided structurally —
   `hash(2, 210, y) === hash(3, 211, y)` because seed⊕x matched — and seed 3 reused seed 2's storm cell verbatim.
   The shipped mix has 0 collisions in 245,760 draws (birthday expectation ~7). T1's noise has its own hash; the
   lab now has two. M1 may want to unify them (both are tiny and private, so this is cosmetic).

## Known issues

- The mean coverage tracks the param best in the mid range (0.2–0.6: within 0.03); at the extremes the brief's own
  formula compresses it (0.95 → 0.816 for the patchy field, since n ≤ 1 caps the mean at 0.945; stratus@0.95
  reaches 0.94 because the deck hugs the top). That's the formula's shape, not the calibration's.
- A regen with a forcedCell costs ~1.5 ms (the percentile sort); idle regens are zero. Nothing to do unless T7
  objects to a 1.5 ms main-thread blip once per second during `cloudAtSun`.
- The stratus deck's variation band (±0.09σ ≈ 0.66–1.0 at 0.95) is a starting point; T8 owns the coverage mapping
  if the overcast reads too flat or too lumpy.

  forcedCell.
- **T5** can rely on: the forcedCell reaches exactly 1.0 at its centre texel (place it on a texel centre for

  `setParams` regenerates whenever its (u, v, radius) move — which will be every second while it tracks the sun.
