# T5 hand-off

Status: done (the e2e check runs once M1 has merged T2 and removed `VOL_TEMP_NOISE`; see "Known issues")

## What I built

The background behind the clouds, and `cloudAtSun`.

- **`background(rd)`** draws the gradient, the stars, the moon and the sun in linear HDR. It ports the look of the
  home page (`bodies.ts`, `particles.ts`, `sky-shader.ts` and `model.ts`) from screen space onto the sky dome. The
  clouds composite over it as `final = c.rgb + c.a × background(rd)` (T0's `targets.ts`, unchanged). So the disc
  disappears where a cloud is dense, glows through a thin edge, and gets its silver lining from T4's phase term.
  - **Gradient:**
    - The zenith, mid and horizon colours by elevation (T0's curve).
    - New: the horizon glow under the sun at sunrise and sunset. It is `horizon × 1.331` at `0.75 · glow`, where
      glow is `model.ts`'s curve: −8° to −2° in, 4° to 10° out. The glow is about 76° wide and 25° tall, the home
      page's 0.9 vw × 0.42 vh ellipse.
    - `background()` dithers it with blue noise (±½ LSB in display space). `skyGradient()` stays dither-free, as T4
      asked, because aerial perspective calls it.
  - **Stars:**
    - The field is a cube-mapped grid, 26 cells per face edge. Each cell holds a star with a probability
      proportional to its solid angle (`(1 + u² + v²)^−1.5` × 0.9). That gives an even ~2,100 over the sphere, or
      ~260 in a 1440×900 view, as on the home page. The field is seeded by `uStarSeed` (the day).
    - The home page's power law: alpha `0.22 + 0.78·m`, radius 0.4–1.4 px at 900 px tall, `m = u^3.2`. 8% are warm
      (`uStarWarm`), the rest cool. Each twinkles at `0.85 + 0.15·sin`, 0.6–2.2 Hz.
    - The field turns about the celestial pole (24.9° N) with the sun's hour angle, so it follows the lab's clock.
    - It is hidden by day (`uStarAlpha`) and faded in the last ~6° above the horizon.
  - **Moon:**
    - The disc is a sphere lit from the sun's side, with the phase angle taken from `uMoonFraction`
      (`cos α = 2f − 1`) and the lit side turned toward the real sun. The terminator is therefore the correct
      ellipse, tilted correctly. At 20:30 on 2026-12-15 it is a 35% crescent lit from below right.
    - Colours: the lit part is `--text` at 0.92, the earthshine 0.06, and a soft glow of 0.16 × illumination (a
      7.3° gaussian, the sky shader's colour).
    - Night only (`model.ts`'s night curve), as on the home page.
  - **Sun:**
    - The disc is limb-darkened with the home page's stops (a hot centre, 82% at the limb) and coloured by
      `uSunColor`, at gain 1.25.
    - Three soft blooms, at 3×, 8× and 20× the radius. They are tinted toward the horizon colour at golden hour and
      breathe ±4% over 9 s.
    - The home page's corona and crepuscular wedges are not ported. Real shafts belong to the volume.
  - **Blending:** stars, the moon and the disc are composited "over" in display (gamma) space, as the home page's
    canvas blends, so their alphas read the same. The blooms add in linear, with the sRGB alphas converted (`a^2.2`).
  - **Sizes:** the sun's radius is 1.07° and the moon's 0.93°: the home page's 16 px and 14 px at 900 px tall under
    the 60° FOV.
- **`forcedCellFor`** (`sun-cell.ts`):
  - The target is the sun while it is above 0.5°, otherwise the moon, otherwise nothing.
  - The cell is centred where the body's ray reaches 2.2 km on the spherical shell. Its radius is a quarter of that
    point's ground distance, clamped to 5–8 km.
  - `edge` moves the centre sideways (perpendicular to the body's bearing) by 0.4 radii.
  - The result is converted to map uv with T2's convention (`c = xz / WEATHER_MAP_KM − offset`, wrapped).
- **The renderer wiring** was already in place in T0's `renderer.ts`: `forcedCellFor` runs on every `setState` and
  once a second. `renderer.ts` is unchanged.

## Decisions (for M1/T8 to carry into DECISIONS.md)

1. **The e2e check measures state A's sun, not B's.**
   - B's sun is 3.4° up, so its ray crosses ~37 km of the cumulus layer, and at cov 0.4 (and even at 0.02) a band
     of low cloud along the horizon already hides it, with transmittance ≈ 0.01, with cloudAtSun off.
   - So B with and without cloudAtSun differ only in the cell's own shape, and a drop can't be measured there.
   - A's sun (40° up) is bright with the cell off, so the ≥ 85% drop is meaningful.
2. **The drop is measured in linear luminance**, not sRGB. It is radiance, so it doesn't depend on the display curve.
3. **The cell's minimum radius is 5 km.** At 3 km the covered base still scattered enough forward light to stop at
   an 85–86% drop: a flaky pass. At 5 km it is 90–91% on both tiers. 7 km was not better.
4. **The home page's alphas are composited in gamma space**, as its canvas does. Linear "over" made the earthshine
   and the faint stars roughly 2–4× too bright.
5. **The horizon glow sits in `skyGradient`.** So aerial perspective toward a low sun picks up the warm haze. It is
   one `exp` per call, and zero work outside golden hour.
6. **The star field turns with the sun's hour angle** (no new uniform), so it is fixed to solar rather than sidereal
   time. That is a 4-minute-a-day difference, and the field is re-seeded daily anyway.
7. **The moon follows the home page: it is drawn at night only.** A daytime moon would be more real; that is T8's
   call.
8. **Sources:** the hash is D. Hoskins's "Hash without Sine" (MIT licence), and is credited in the shader. The rest
   is from the home page's own code and the geometry. No other code was ported.

## Known issues

- **The e2e check can't run on this branch alone.** It needs T2's `forcedCell` (the placeholder map ignores it) and
  M1's removal of `VOL_TEMP_NOISE` (the temporary noise overrides the map's coverage).
  - The spec checks both, skips with the reason, and runs by itself once both are in.
  - I ran it against the overlay (T1 + T2 + the define off) with `pnpm build:e2e`: it passed (1.0 s).
- **State B's acceptance item 5 ("sun hidden where covered, bleeding through thin edges") can't show at B as
  configured.** The horizon cloud band hides the low sun whatever `cloudAtSun` says.
  - The fix is T2's coverage or T4's or T8's near-horizon density, not the background.
  - Alternatively, a forced clear gap (see Requests).
- **The sun disc at gain 1.25 washes toward pale yellow** in its hot centre at low elevation: the centre's +35% is
  mixed in linear here. T8 may lower `BACKGROUND_SUN_GAIN` or make the hot-centre mix gamma-space.
- **The cell is anchored to the body while the field drifts past it**, re-placed once a second. That is fine for the
  lab, but it is visibly stationary in a long run without `freeze`.
- **The 40 km map tiles**, so the forced cell repeats every 40 km (copies elsewhere in the sky at low elevations).
- **No mobile-specific star boost** (the home page uses alpha ≥ 0.35 and 1.25× size below 808 px). The shader doesn't
  know the CSS width.

## Requests for M1

1. **Optional: a forced clear gap.** `WeatherMapParams.forcedCell` could take `clear?: number`, a radius in uv

   half behind a cloud with clear sky beside it, even at low elevations, where B's horizon band otherwise hides it.
   This is T2's file, so it is a request, not a change.
