## What I changed (constants and shaping functions only)

- **Density (`density.glsl.ts`):**
  - Stratus samples SHAPE at a 3.2-km tile on axes turned by ~37°, mixed with 20% of the 10-km field. Past 12–35 km
    (ground distance) it fades back to the 10-km field (F3).
  - Erosion 0.6 → 0.7 (cumulus), 0.22 → 0.4 (stratus).
  - Coverage floor: weather coverage below 0.07 makes no cloud, full strength at 0.22. It removes the dust of tiny
    fragments between clusters.
  - The height profile is offset by ±0.035 of the shape's low fbm, so bases aren't ruler-flat.
- **Lighting (`lighting.glsl.ts`):**
  - Light-path extinction ×0.55 → ×0.65. First multiple-scattering octave weight 0.5 → 0.3.
  - Base ambient: 70% zenith colour mixed into the horizon colour. Dusk bodies are violet-grey, not brown.
  - Diffuse transmission: 0.35 → 0.22 for cumulus, 1.0 for a stratus deck (an infinite slab), with a half-neutral
    tint. Midday overcast is luminous grey, not storm-dark or sepia.
  - Aerial perspective: 55 → 32 km. Above 60–95% coverage the haze greys toward the horizon's luminance. The blue
    strip under the overcast deck is gone.
- **March (`march.glsl.ts`):** the detail LOD fades at 15 → 50 km for stratus (moiré spokes) and stays at 25 → 90 km
  for cumulus. Without erosion, backlit cumulus near a low sun turn into thick glowing outlines.
- **Background (`background.glsl.ts`):** sun gain 1.25 → 2.6, inner bloom 0.099 → 0.22, middle bloom 0.0094 → 0.015.
  The disc burns out to a hot yellow-white like a photograph, not a flat peach disc.
- **F2: cloudAtSun=edge (`sun-cell.ts`, `weather-map.ts`, `WeatherMapParams.forcedCell` in `types.ts`):**
  - "edge" is now one cumulus-sized cell (3.2 km, shifted 0.8 radii sideways), not the 5–8 km coverage disc.
  - A clear wedge runs along the body's line of sight, from its base crossing to its top crossing. Its half-width is
    0.8 km + tan 3° × distance.
  - Before this, B's 3.4° sun crossed ~38 km of random cumulus and was always hidden.
  - "cover" is unchanged.
- **Tests:**
  - `lab-cloud-at-sun.spec.ts` gains state B.
  - `vol-sun-cell.test.ts` measures the edge shift against the edge cell's own radius. That file is T5's, so this is
    flagged for M1.

## What still doesn't look real (honest list)

1. **A's edges are soft.**
   - Fair-weather cumulus in a photo have crisp, fine-grained edges. Ours read as cotton wool, because the clouds
     render at 0.35 × the drawing buffer (T7) and the bilateral upsample smooths what's left.
   - The fix is outside T8: a higher `TIERS.scale`, or sharpening in the upsample (`REPROJECT_SHARPNESS`, T7).
   - Both cost GPU time the desktop budget doesn't have.
2. **A's clouds are grey.**
   - The camera faces the sun, so every cloud is backlit: dark bodies with bright rims. That's physically right, but
     the shot never shows the brilliant white sunlit side people picture.
   - `view=` can turn the camera away from the sun. The preset doesn't.
3. **C, overhead:**
   - Around the bright thin patch near the sun, the deck has soft radial streaks.
   - At full resolution, faint spokes remain near the horizon, right of centre.
   - Both come from SHAPE having no usable mips (`MIN_FILTER` is LINEAR, T1), so far samples alias. Making the noise
     textures `LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` (T1) would let `textureLod` pick coarser levels and should remove them.
4. **B, the near-horizon band:**
   - At the 0.35 scale, small bright bits under the sun show stair-steps at DPR 1. At DPR 2 and 3 they are mostly
     hidden.
   - Some lit cloud bases still run long and straight.
5. **B, the edge's look depends on the noise.**
   - The disc's centre loses 50–93% of its light, depending on the tier: the noise tile differs (64³ against 128³),
     and so do the steps.
   - As the field drifts (unfrozen), the cloud's edge boils across the disc. That's realistic, but no two frames match.
6. **The clear wedge (`cloudAtSun=edge`) is a staging device.**
   - It's ~38 km long on a 40-km tiling map, so its copies appear as thin clear lanes at other azimuths near the
     horizon. They are faint after the haze, but they are there.
   - It only exists in the lab's cloudAtSun mode.
7. **The sun disc is the home page's 1.07° stylised size** (about twice the real one). It now burns out like a
   photograph, but its size is a design choice, not physics.

## Decisions (carried into `docs/DECISIONS.md` #195–#197; T7's D1–D8 summarised as #194)

- **D1:** "edge" is one cumulus-sized cell plus a clear wedge along the line of sight (F2).
- **D2:** a stratus deck uses a separate, rotated 3.2-km SHAPE tile that fades out with distance (F3). Diffuse
  transmission is per type: 1.0 for stratus, 0.22 for cumulus.
- **D3:** the B e2e reads the "transmittance" debug view, not display luminance:
  - The edge's forward-scattering glow is as bright as the disc, so luminance can't tell covered from clear.
  - It checks: off < 0.1 at the centre (the field hides it), the far limb > 0.5 (the wedge clears it), and the
    centre < 0.85 (the cell is in front).
  - F2's "the centre loses 30–85%" holds on the low tier only. On medium and high it loses up to 93%.
