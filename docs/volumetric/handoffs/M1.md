## Per task

### T1: noise
- **Checked:** `createNoiseTextures` follows the contract (SHAPE 64³/128³ RGBA8, DETAIL 32³, BLUE 64×64 R8 NEAREST,
  every texture REPEAT). It saves and restores the GL state around generation. Overlapping calls (StrictMode) share
  one refcounted run. `genMs` waits for completion through a `readPixels`. `?debug=noise` is registered through
  `debug-views.ts`.
- **Tests (59):** meaningful. They cover tileability inside the tile and across the lattice wrap, range and
  histogram shape per channel, hash uniformity, the recipe limits (powers of two, 4 texels per cell), recipe↔GLSL
  agreement, the blue-noise spectrum and seam, and the viewer layout. Gap: GPU-vs-CPU agreement can't run in vitest
  (F4).
- **Fixed:** nothing in code. Docs: `types.ts` now says the mip chains exist (request 4).
- **Nit, left:** the viewer's `--ink` fallback is spelled as `0x0b/0x12/0x20` literals. It is only a fallback for a
  token read that fails, and it isn't a CSS hex string.

### T2: weather map
- **Checked:** the generator is deterministic and seeded. The field is percentile-calibrated (cumulus) and z-scored
  (stratus). The map tiles on the torus. A forced cell reaches ≥ 0.95 at its centre and wraps. The wind is correct
  (from 270 → +u). Only field params regenerate the map; the wind only scrolls it.
- **Tests (30):** meaningful. They cover determinism, the mean windows, patchiness against a uniform deck, seam
  smoothness, cumulonimbus cells, precipitation concentration, the forced-cell wrap, the scroll direction, and
  regeneration.
