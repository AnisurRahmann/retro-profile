# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads, and recruiters evaluating a senior/staff backend & AI engineer. They scan fast, looking for impact signals and skillset fit. Context: they have 20 other tabs open, 30 seconds per portfolio. The job to be done is "is this engineer worth a conversation?"

## Product Purpose

Shakil's personal portfolio — a vertical-scroll "reels" experience that presents engineering impact as a narrative. Every section leads with a number. The site replaces the traditional resume PDF with something more memorable and more honest: a scrollable case study of shipped work.

## Brand Personality

Confident, direct, results-driven. No hedging, no "I helped," no passive voice. Every claim has a metric attached. The voice is an engineer talking to engineers, not a job seeker performing for HR.

## Anti-references

- No SaaS corporate polish: soft gradients, friendly illustrations, "we" language, or can-do optimism
- No startup bro culture: memes, casual tone, emoji-heavy content
- No generic developer portfolio: boring grids, stock icons, or safe blue/white themes

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with the number.** $900K saved, $1M+ GMV, 2,500 posts/hr. Metrics before adjectives.
2. **Respect the scanner.** Reels format = fast vertical consumption. Each screen makes one point. No walls of text.
3. **Expert confidence.** Bold serif typography, dark canvas, minimal decoration. The work speaks; the frame gets out of the way.
4. **No filler.** Every word earns its place. No intros that repeat the heading. No decorative elements that don't carry information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Sufficient contrast on all text. Keyboard-navigable reel scrolling. Focus indicators visible. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.
